Meet Squibs

Hello! my name is Anton, I'm a multimedia designer based in Aarhus, Denmark. I'm currently studying at EAAA - Erhvervsakademiet Aarhus, where I'm studying to become a professional designer. I'm incredibly passionate about creating visually pleasing intuitive design, that draws the user in and leaves them with an impression.

Projects

  • Lumina One

    My first semester exam project, where I was tasked with creating a website for a fictional company. The companies name is Lumina and their main product was to create a small handy but stylish bluetooth speaker. My plan was to create a website that was simple and intuitive, but also relatable to a younger audience, while styling it in a luxurious yet relaxed way.

    Visit Lumina One ↗
  • Børnenes bogunivers / Childrens book universe

    this project for my second semester exam i had to develop a website for a fictional company, this time the company was called "Børnenes bogunivers" or "Childrens book universe" in english. The purpose of the company was to create a website where children would get drawn in and potentially find a book they would want to read, while keeping the website intuitive and easy to navigate for both children and adults. Other than that it should also be possible to donate your old books to the company, so they could be resold and given a new home.
