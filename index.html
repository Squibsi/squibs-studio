Meet Squibs

Hello! My name is Anton, I’m a multimedia designer currently based in Aarhus, Denmark. I’m currently studying multimedia design at EAAA - Erhvervsakademi Aarhus. I'm eager to learn and grow as a designer, and I’m passionate about creating visually compelling, intuitive designs that draw people in and leave a lasting impression.

Projects

  • Lumina One

    My first semester exam project, where I was tasked with creating a website for a fictional company. The company's name is Lumina and their main product was to create a small handy but stylish Bluetooth speaker. My plan was to create a website that was simple and intuitive, but also relatable to a younger audience, while styling it in a luxurious yet relaxed way.

    HTML, CSS, JS

    Visit Lumina One ↗
  • Børnenes bogunivers / Children's book universe

    This project for my second semester exam I had to develop a website for a fictional company, this time the company was called "Børnenes bogunivers" or "Childrens book universe" in English. The purpose of the company was to create a website where children would get drawn in and potentially find a book they would want to read, while keeping the website intuitive and easy to navigate for both children and adults. Other than that it should also be possible to donate your old books to the company, so they could be resold and given a new home.

    WORDPRESS

    Visit Børnenes bogunivers | Children's book universe ↗
  • Ovartaci Muséum

    The Ovartaci project was made for a real client, the Ovartaci Muséum in Aarhus, Denmark. It was still part of my second semester exam. The task was to create an interactive experience the museum visitors could engage in while visiting the museum. My group took an angle that lets you dive into the mind of the artist/patient Ovartaci and lead you on a treasure hunt through the museum to gather his mind, considering he was a patient at the psychiatric hospital in Aarhus, and wasn't shy about it either, which you can easily see through his art and writings.

    HTML, CSS, JS
