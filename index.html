Meet Squibs

Hello! My name is Anton, I’m a multimedia designer currently based in Aarhus, Denmark. I’m currently studying multimedia design at EAAA – Erhvervsakademi Aarhus. I’m eager to learn and grow as a designer, and I’m passionate about creating visually compelling, intuitive designs that draw people in and leave a lasting impression.

Projects

  • Lumina One

    My first semester exam project, where I was tasked with creating a website for a fictional company. The company’s name is Lumina, and its main product was a small, handy but stylish Bluetooth speaker. My plan was to create a website that was simple and intuitive, but also relatable to a younger audience, while styling it in a luxurious yet relaxed way.

    HTML, CSS, JS

    Visit Lumina One ↗
  • Børnenes Bogunivers | Children’s Book Universe

    For this second-semester exam project, I had to develop a website for a fictional company called “Børnenes Bogunivers,” or “Children’s Book Universe” in English. The purpose was to create a website that would draw children in and help them find a book they might want to read, while keeping it intuitive and easy to navigate for both children and adults. Visitors could also donate old books so they could be resold and given a new home.

    WordPress

    Visit Børnenes Bogunivers ↗
  • Ovartaci Museum

    The Ovartaci project was created for a real client, the Ovartaci Museum in Aarhus, Denmark, as part of my second-semester exam. The task was to create an interactive experience visitors could engage with while exploring the museum. My group developed an experience that invites visitors into the mind of the artist and patient Ovartaci, leading them on a treasure hunt through the museum to gather fragments of his mind. His experience as a psychiatric patient is visible throughout his art and writing.

    HTML, CSS, JS
